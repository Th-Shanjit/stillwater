# Product-Led Distribution

## Core thesis

Marketing should be a consequence of meaningful product use.

Give people something worth hosting, experiencing
and inviting others into.

Do not build an open social network merely to acquire users.

## Send somebody a moment

Shareable, useful browser-based experiences.

Examples:
- A short grounding exercise.
- A quiet evening practice.
- A moment of encouragement.
- A guided prayer.

No installation required before receiving value.

Shared links must not contain private recovery information.

## Walk with me

Invite a friend into a voluntary shared journey.

Participants share an experience, not private recovery records.

Easy refusal and departure are mandatory.

## Stillwater Host

Enable ordinary adults and ministry leaders to facilitate
small-group programmes.

Proposed features:
- Ready-to-use session guides.
- Group audio and visual exercises.
- Practical activities.
- Optional discussion prompts.
- Invitation links and QR codes.
- Promotional materials.
- Shared scheduling.
- Confidentiality and safeguarding guidance.

Basic hosting should remain free.

## Church programmes

A church can provide a voluntary programme to its congregation.

Explore:
- Sunday-to-Sunday experiences.
- Congregation-wide seasonal journeys.
- Branded programme pages.
- Sponsored membership access.

No reporting of private recovery behaviour to religious leaders.

Do not turn Stillwater into a church administration suite.

## Creator-led journeys

Offer Christian creators their own interactive programmes.

Creators contribute original content, voice and expertise.

Stillwater provides the programme engine and distribution tools.

Start with manually curated partnerships.

Potential niches:
- Christian lifestyle.
- Relationships and marriage.
- Digital wellbeing.
- Recovery-focused creators.
- Pastoral educators.
- Christian contemplative practices.

Future Creator Studio may provide self-service tools
after demand and content governance are validated.

## Seasonal distribution

Explore coordinated experiences around:

- New Year.
- Lent.
- Advent.
- Other appropriate Christian occasions.

Respect denominational differences.

## Sharing incentives

- Shared participation.
- Gift passes.
- Sponsored access.
- Becoming a host.
- Sharing useful experiences.

No coercive referrals or recruitment leaderboards.

## Acquisition metrics

- Invitation-to-activation.
- Unique participants per host.
- Programme completion.
- Hosts returning to run another programme.
- Participants becoming hosts.
- Paid conversion.
- Acquisition cost.
- Refunds and retention.

Measure audience overlap and incremental acquisition.

Community participation is not proof of recovery.
