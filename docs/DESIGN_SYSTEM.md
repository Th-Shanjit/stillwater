# Stillwater Design Direction

Status: awaiting Part 01 design decisions.

## Visual concept

Atmospheric water-inspired design combined with modern,
functional recovery interfaces.

Use restrained sacred symbolism where meaningful.

Avoid making the entire application resemble a digital
devotional book or a collection of religious quotations.

## Visual principles

- Calm without becoming visually empty.
- Meaningful imagery instead of ornamental clutter.
- Clear information hierarchy.
- Large, obvious primary actions.
- Limited reading during difficult moments.
- Natural transitions and restrained animation.
- Accessible contrast and typography.
- Reduced-motion alternatives.
- Light and dark environments.

## Interaction principles

Every screen must answer:

1. What is happening?
2. What should the user do next?
3. Can the user skip or leave?
4. Is the interaction accessible?
5. Does this improve real-world outcomes?

Do not introduce interaction solely to increase engagement.

## Planned design system

- Semantic colour tokens.
- Typography hierarchy.
- Spacing and layout tokens.
- Surface and elevation rules.
- Buttons and interactive cards.
- Navigation components.
- Progress visualisations.
- Breathing and grounding animations.
- Audio-capable guided practice components.
- Empty, loading and error states.
- Shared programme and host components.

## Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.2 AA where applicable.

Support text scaling, screen readers, reduced motion,
clear focus order and large touch targets.

Audio must have readable transcripts or equivalent guidance.
Colour must not be the sole carrier of information.

## Content voice

Direct, compassionate, grounded and concise.

Avoid unsupported psychological interpretations,
shame, forced reassurance and theological lecturing.

Exact palette, typography and motion specifications
will be established in Part 01.
