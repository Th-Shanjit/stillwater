# Part 05C - Personal Sanctuary

## Purpose

An optional quiet space, available free from the
first visit. It requires no practice or return history.

## Visual environment

Layered SVG landscape:
- Sky and celestial light
- Distant mountains
- Lakeside hills
- Water and reflections
- Foreground trees and stones
- Restrained Christian symbolism

Daylight and moonlight are freely selectable.

The preview and dedicated screen reuse the same
landscape component.

## Interaction

Users may visit, switch scenery, open a quiet prayer
or leave whenever they choose.

No timers, streaks, scores or compulsory tasks.

No visit, duration or prayer interaction is recorded.

The scene selection is temporary for this milestone.

## Future growth

05D will introduce permanent, additive scene layers.

A setback or absence must never remove an unlocked
environmental feature.

The base scene must remain complete and welcoming.

## Production artwork backlog

The SVG is an initial functional illustration.

Later production may replace individual layers with
original textured artwork while preserving dimensions,
layer boundaries, accessibility and performance.

Audio and ambient recordings remain deferred.

No new dependencies, remote assets or database changes.

## Validation

Check route registration, daylight/moonlight rendering,
screen-reader descriptions, larger text, scrolling,
status-bar contrast and Android hardware back.

Native rendering tests remain required.
