# Part 06B - Interactive Programme Player

The player renders one programme step at a time
using the Stillwater V2 design system.

All five step kinds are supported.

Suggested durations are advisory.
No countdown or automatic advancement is required.

Prayer and reflection may be skipped.
Users may navigate backwards or leave at any point.

A review screen precedes explicit session completion.

Session results distinguish continued and skipped
steps. They do not claim actions were performed.

The player returns a day ID and step dispositions.
It captures no private reflection text.

Parents must remount the player for a different
programme edition or day.

The development preview uses Three Quiet Evenings.
It is not a production enrolment flow.

No database records, recovery events, sanctuary growth,
audio playback or participation sharing are generated.

Audio asset references retain a text fallback.
Actual media integration remains deferred.

Leaving the preview discards its in-memory session.

06C introduces the programme library and enrolment.
06D introduces durable participation and resumption.

Native rendering and accessibility tests are pending.
