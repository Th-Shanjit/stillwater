# Product Decision Register

## Established working direction

- Action-first Christian recovery companion.
- Faith as foundation rather than mandatory workload.
- Specific private recovery experience with broader,
  transparent shared intentional-living programmes.
- No public social feed initially.
- No unrestricted recovery chatbot initially.
- No native blocker in initial scope.
- No competitive recovery mechanisms.
- No inferred abstinence.
- Essential support and privacy remain free.
- Local-first private recovery data.
- Future connected programme data remains separate.
- Curated creators before an open marketplace.
- Basic hosting and sharing remain free.
- Lifetime purchase boundaries disclosed before purchase.
- Recordings produced after application workflows are built.

## Foundation implementation decisions

- Preserve historical database records.
- Count recorded practices and returns.
- Remove unsupported protection-rate claims.
- Authentication exceptions must not unlock the app.
- Do not persist new raw laments.
- Remove nonfunctional icon selection.
- Distinguish audio playback from timed-text guidance.
- Remove unconditional free-trial advertising.
- Show actual store prices.
- Remove onboarding purchase gate.
- Retain local Grace Scholarship provisionally.

## Deferred or unresolved

- Exact visual palette and typography.
- Final navigation details.
- Clinical and pastoral content validation.
- Notification consent and lifecycle management.
- Database encryption and backup policy.
- Local data deletion interface.
- Complete authentication lifecycle.
- Programme schema and backend selection.
- Church and creator governance.
- Final prices.
- Subscription and lifetime purchase tests.
- Grace Scholarship sustainability.
- Audio production and licensing.

## Evidence standard

Distinguish researched mechanisms, product hypotheses
and verified implementation results.

Do not treat competitor adoption as proof that
the same feature will succeed in Stillwater.
