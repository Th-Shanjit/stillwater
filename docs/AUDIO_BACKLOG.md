Missing recordings must not block application development.

Do not commission recordings or purchase voice-generation
services until the corresponding experiences are finalised.

## Voice direction

Natural, steady, compassionate and clear.

Avoid theatrical sermons, exaggerated whispering,
dramatic emotional manipulation and forced reassurance.

## Production sequence

1. Finish application workflows.
2. Finalise guided experiences.
3. Review scripts for safety and theological suitability.
4. Select appropriate voices.
5. Record or generate approved audio.
6. Edit, master and verify licensing.
7. Integrate assets through stable content IDs.
8. Test playback, interruptions and accessibility.

Audio-only controls should activate only when
appropriate audio is available.

Keep functional text and visual alternatives.

## Deferred content expansion
