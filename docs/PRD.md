# Stillwater Product Requirements

Status: V2 working specification.

## Primary problem

During difficult moments, people may have limited attention,
motivation and capacity for lengthy reflection.

Stillwater should reduce the effort required to interrupt
unwanted behaviour and choose a practical next action.

The existing application is too dependent on reading,
self-analysis and devotional interpretation.

## Research personas

These are hypothetical personas, not interviewed participants.

### Daniel

23, experiences late-night urges.

Needs immediate support, low cognitive burden and
a practical environmental change.

### Michael

36, struggles with shame after setbacks.

Needs a constructive next step without punishment
or mandatory confession.

### Sarah

28, privacy-conscious and concerned about judgement.

Needs discreet interfaces and control over disclosure.

### Thomas

42, focused on long-term maintenance.

Needs useful routines, preparation and accurate patterns.

### Esther

21, vulnerable to religious anxiety and reassurance rituals.

Needs optional spiritual practices without compulsive
checking, repeated confession or moral scoring.

## Additional user segments

- Trusted friends and supporters.
- Small-group leaders.
- Pastors and ministry teams.
- Christian creators.
- Sponsors and ministry partners.

## Core recovery cycle

Prepare -> Interrupt -> Redirect -> Return -> Learn.

### Prepare

Suggest one practical action before a difficult period.

Use editable if-then plans, reminders and previously
reported helpful actions.

Do not claim to predict setbacks.

### Interrupt

Provide one-tap access to immediate support.

Offer an immediate practical action and optional
breathing, visual guidance, haptics or audio.

No mandatory writing or emotional classification.

### Redirect

Help the user change their environment, move away
from the screen or voluntarily contact a trusted person.

Encourage returning to everyday life.

### Return

After a setback, prioritise interrupting further
unwanted behaviour and making one practical change.

Reflection and prayer remain optional.

### Learn

Show recorded events and voluntarily confirmed outcomes.

Never infer abstinence or recovery from missing records.

## Proposed navigation

Today:
- Immediate help above the fold.
- Start Again.
- One recommended preparation action.
- Access to personal plans.

Journey:
- Guided experiences.
- Structured recovery programmes.
- Optional spiritual practices.
- Shared programmes when available.

Progress:
- Recorded actions.
- Personal patterns with limitations.
- Optional visual sanctuary.

Settings:
- Privacy and authentication.
- Notifications.
- Accessibility.
- Spiritual preferences.
- Local data management.

Circles may become a separate destination after
shared functionality justifies its own tab.

## Visual sanctuary

Optional, persistent representation of constructive effort.

No loss of accumulated scenery after setbacks.
No competitive rankings, currencies or loot boxes.
No obligation to interact daily.

## Shared programme experience

Use one reusable engine supporting:

- Visual exercises.
- Timed text.
- Audio.
- Practical actions.
- Optional reflection.
- Prayer.
- Shared schedules.
- Invitations.

Programme participation must not expose personal
recovery activity.

## Acceptance hypotheses

- Immediate support accessible within two seconds
  from an already-open home screen.
- First practical action available within 30 seconds.
- Short intervention achievable within 90 seconds.
- No mandatory writing during immediate support.
- No payment requirement for essential support.

These are design targets, not clinical effectiveness claims.
