# Commercial Strategy

Status: hypotheses requiring validation.

## Free personal experience

- Essential immediate support.
- Post-setback support.
- Basic preventive planning.
- Basic progress.
- Privacy and authentication.
- Trusted-contact functionality.
- Basic shared participation and invitations.

Do not place a purchase barrier in an urgent intervention.

## Personal Plus

Potential benefits:
- Extended structured programmes.
- Additional guided practices and recordings.
- Advanced planning and accurate insights.
- Optional sanctuary customisation.

Only advertise implemented benefits.

## Proposed price experiments

Monthly: USD 5.99.
Annual: USD 34.99.
Founding lifetime: USD 39.99.
Standard lifetime: USD 69.99.

These are hypotheses, not configured store prices.

Display real localised store prices at purchase time.

Do not promise an introductory trial without
verifying the applicable offer.

## Lifetime entitlement

Core local premium features and defined updates.

Future hosted services and separately licensed content
may carry separate charges if clearly disclosed.

Do not redefine purchased entitlements retroactively.

## Host and church revenue

Explore optional paid conveniences and custom programmes.

Illustrative annual range: USD 49-299.

Basic hosting and sharing should remain free.

## Creator revenue

Explore:
- Licensed original content.
- Transparent revenue sharing.
- Co-branded programmes.
- Premium standalone journeys.

Illustrative programme price: USD 4.99-19.99.

Avoid financial incentives that encourage religious
leaders to pressure vulnerable participants.

## Grace Scholarship

Existing local hardship access is retained.

Current implementation grants a renewable 365-day pass.

Review sustainability and potential abuse before launch.

Explore sponsored access funded by churches,
creators and individual donors.

Never disclose recipient recovery information to donors.

## Billing architecture

Retain RevenueCat.

Use verified store entitlement information and
supported SDK caching.

Do not maintain an indefinitely valid paid-access
boolean as an independent subscription authority.

Test renewal, cancellation, expiry, restoration,
refunds, lifetime access and offline behaviour.

## Commercial design

Allow users to experience the product before purchase.

No mandatory onboarding purchase decision.
No ads or sensitive-data advertising.
No misleading scarcity or recovery guarantees.
