## Recording direction

- Calm natural delivery.
- No exaggerated whispering or theatrical preaching.
- No promises of recovery or fixed urge duration.
- No pressure to repeat prayers or achieve certainty.
- Optional religious language where appropriate.
- Comfortable pauses and intelligible speech.
- No unlicensed music, voices or ambience.

## Production requirements

Each recording requires:

- Approved script.
- Stable content identifier.
- Voice and recording rights.
- Final transcript.
- Verified asset duration.
- Corresponding timed-text cues.
- Offline playback test.
- Pause, interruption and resume test.
- Accessible non-audio alternative.

## Production order

before adding new audio-enabled experiences.
