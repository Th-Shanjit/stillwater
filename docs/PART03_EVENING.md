# Part 03D - Evening Wind-down

## Experience

Practical bedtime preparation comes first.

Optional experiences:
- Silent gratitude reflection.
- Structured reflection tags.
- Three-cycle breathing practice.
- Prayer respecting the selected prayer tradition.
- Longer guided Examen.

All may be skipped.

The user explicitly records completion.
Closing without saving does not create a completion.

## Persistence

The evening routine preserves the existing daily_logs
schema and examen_completed field.

Existing gratitude text is never overwritten.

Skipping reflection preserves previously recorded tags.
Explicit new selections replace the previous tags.

Morning practice flags and recorded returns are preserved.

The practice date is captured when the screen opens
to avoid changing the intended day during a midnight rollover.

Completion is not evidence of sleep quality,
abstinence or a successful recovery outcome.

## Content access

The basic evening routine remains free.

The longer evening text practice is made available
inside this routine without a payment requirement.
Its legacy library access tier remains unchanged
until the commercial consolidation phase.
