# Parts 09A, 09B and 09C

## Free and Plus

Essential immediate support remains free.

The immediate post-setback guided practice has
been moved from Plus into the free tier.

The complete 30-day recovery path and additional
optional guided practices remain Plus benefits.

The currently implemented personal planning,
privacy, Compass, Sanctuary, basic programmes
and basic hosting remain free.

## Purchased access

RevenueCat remains the authority for purchased
Plus entitlements.

Store subscription validity is not independently
overridden by an indefinitely stored local flag.

An active store entitlement without an expiry
date is displayed without assuming the exact
purchase or promotional product that granted it.

Lifetime products must be configured as genuine
non-consumable purchases in the original store.

## Purchase presentation

The membership screen uses store-localised prices.

Unavailable or mismatched billing packages are
excluded from the purchase options.

No trial, saving percentage or specific renewal
offer is promised without store confirmation.

Purchase and restoration operations share
an in-process transaction lock.

Privacy policy and terms URLs are required
before the purchase button is enabled.

The URL check validates format only.
Deployment and legal review remain required.

## Grace Scholarship

The existing renewable local 365-day hardship
pass remains available.

It is not described as a store subscription or
a verified lifetime purchase.

Its commercial sustainability remains under
review. Existing access is not removed.

## Lifetime scope

Core local Plus features are the intended scope.

Future separately licensed content and hosted
services are not automatically bundled.

Do not retroactively narrow previously purchased
entitlement promises.
