# Stillwater V2

## Product identity

Stillwater is an action-first Christian recovery companion
for adults seeking support with unwanted compulsive digital
sexual habits.

It is not a Bible study application, a diagnostic tool,
a surveillance product or a substitute for professional care.

Faith is the foundation, not the workload.

The product should help people take meaningful action
in their everyday lives rather than spend more time
inside the application.

## Product ecosystem

### Personal Stillwater

- Immediate support during difficult moments.
- Preventive planning and practical guidance.
- Compassionate post-setback support.
- Guided visual, audio and haptic practices.
- Meaningful, accurately recorded progress.
- Optional personal sanctuary.
- Local-first recovery records.

### Stillwater Together

- Shared intentional-living programmes.
- Walk with me.
- Shareable browser experiences.
- Stillwater Host.
- Church programmes.
- Creator-led journeys.
- Seasonal programmes.

Participation must be voluntary and transparent.
Private recovery records never become group records.

## Product principles

1. Action before explanation.
2. Guide rather than lecture.
3. Reduce cognitive burden.
4. Use visuals and interaction before long paragraphs.
5. Never punish setbacks or inactivity.
6. Never infer abstinence from missing data.
7. Keep essential support and privacy free.
8. Protect sensitive information.
9. Respect different Christian traditions.
10. Build distribution into meaningful functionality.
11. Validate impact rather than claiming effectiveness.
12. Keep recordings separate from application development.
